Statistics · Econometrics · Actuarial Science · Machine Learning — interactive simulations. Turn the knobs, hit RUN, watch results update live.

Module 1 — Two-Sample Comparison ready

Generates two random samples from Normal distributions. Adjust the sliders and see how p-values change.

Press RUN to generate samples and run the test.

Module 2 — Regression: Linear vs. Non-Linear Fit ready

Pick a true underlying shape, then pick a fit degree. Watch R² change as you adjust the fit.

Press GENERATE & FIT to create data and fit a curve.

Module 3 — Normality Check ready

Press GENERATE & TEST to draw a sample and check normality.

Module 1 — Stationarity (Dickey-Fuller) ready

Generates a time series as either a random walk (non-stationary) or a mean-reverting AR(1) process (stationary).

Press GENERATE & TEST to build a series and check stationarity.

Module 2 — Heteroskedasticity (Breusch-Pagan) ready

Fits y = a + bx, then regresses the squared residuals on x. A significant result means residual variance is NOT constant.

Press GENERATE & TEST to check for heteroskedasticity.

Module 3 — Spurious Regression Demo ready

Generates two independent random walks and regresses one on the other — the reason you must check stationarity first.

Press GENERATE & REGRESS to create two unrelated random walks.

Module 1 — Mortality Experience Study ready

Simulates actual deaths as a Poisson process. Computes SMR with confidence interval.

Press SIMULATE & TEST to generate an experience study.

Module 2 — Bühlmann Credibility ready

Enter each group's mean claim, variance, and exposure. Computes credibility factor Z per group.

Press COMPUTE CREDIBILITY to blend each group's experience.

Module 3 — Chain-Ladder Reserve ready

Edit the cumulative-loss triangle. Click COMPUTE to derive development factors and total the IBNR reserve.

Press COMPUTE RESERVE to derive development factors and the IBNR reserve.

Module 1 — Vector Autoregression (VAR) ready

Models the joint dynamics of multiple stationary time series. Each variable depends on its own lags and the lags of others.

Press SIMULATE & ESTIMATE to fit a VAR model.

Module 2 — Cointegration ready

Tests if two non-stationary series share a stable long-run relationship using the Engle-Granger test.

Press GENERATE & TEST to check for cointegration.

Module 3 — Linear Classifier (Logistic Regression) ready

Demonstrates a simple binary classifier. Two classes with different means — the model learns the decision boundary.

Press GENERATE & CLASSIFY to train a linear classifier.
